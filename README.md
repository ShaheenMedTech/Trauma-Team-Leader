## About the Project

**The Trauma Team Leader** is a developing clinical manuscript focused on the practical leadership of major trauma resuscitation.

The project combines trauma assessment and resuscitation with the non-technical skills required to lead a multidisciplinary team effectively in high-pressure environments.

Its focus includes:

- Major trauma assessment and resuscitation
- Trauma team activation and preparation
- Leadership during the primary survey
- Prioritisation and delegation
- Closed-loop communication
- Situational awareness
- Crisis team management
- Human factors and team performance
- Handover, transfer, and debriefing
- Safety and clinical governance

---

## Current Manuscript

The repository currently contains a structured manuscript with chapters covering:

- Major trauma foundations
- `<C>ABCDE` assessment
- Airway, breathing, circulation, neurological and exposure priorities
- Pelvic, extremity, paediatric, pregnancy, and burns trauma
- Trauma team roles and responsibilities
- Trauma team leadership
- Communication and delegation
- Situational awareness
- Human factors
- Error management and safety
- Handover and transfer

The manuscript is currently under active development and restructuring.
